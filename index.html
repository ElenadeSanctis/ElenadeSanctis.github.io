Projects

  • Phase-Field Fracture Propagation

    A C++ solver for brittle fracture that couples elastic equilibrium with a phase-field damage model on a Material Point Method discretisation.

    • Fully explicit, matrix-free scheme: CFL-limited momentum update and damped Jacobi for the phase field, with no global linear solve.
    • Reproduces the single-edge-notched shear test and dynamic crack branching; shear stiffness matches the analytical value to five significant digits.
    C++MPMexplicit solvers
    Ongoing · spring 2026, now being parallelised

  • Smoke Dispersion from a Vehicle Fire

    Steady RANS simulations of smoke from a bus fire on the Linköping University campus to identify the buildings most at risk.

    • 20-million-cell meshes run on the NSC Sigma HPC cluster with scripted Fluent journals.
    • 20 years of hourly wind data reduced to five cases with k-means, combined into probability-weighted exposure maps.
    • Co-authored a scientific paper and presented a poster.
    Ansys FluentMATLABParaViewHPC
    Course project · fall 2026

  • External Aerodynamics of the DrivAer Car

    An automated pipeline from CAD to post-processing for the DrivAer reference vehicle, with moving ground and rotating wheels.

    • Parametric Scheme script for Fluent Meshing producing a 9.3-million-cell poly-hexcore mesh with 10 prism layers.
    • Realizable k–ε RANS at 20 m/s, run in batch on the NSC Sigma cluster; ParaView post-processing in Python.
    ANSAAnsys FluentParaViewPython
    Course project · fall 2026
