External Aerodynamics of the DrivAer Car

An automated CFD pipeline for the DrivAer reference car, with moving ground and rotating wheels.

Institution

Linköping University

Period

Sep — Oct 2026

Compute

NSC Sigma cluster

Overview

The geometry was prepared in ANSA: CAD cleaning, surface meshing and a half-model domain with moving ground, wheel contact patches and three nested refinement regions of 25, 75 and 200 mm.

A parametric Scheme script for Ansys Fluent Meshing automates the volume mesh, with size fields and 10 prism layers built with different methods on the vehicle and on the ground, for a 9.3-million-cell poly-hexcore mesh.

Results

  • Automated the steady RANS simulation (realizable k–ε, 20 m/s, rotating wheels) in Fluent, run in batch mode on the NSC Sigma cluster.
  • Automated the post-processing in ParaView through Python scripts.
