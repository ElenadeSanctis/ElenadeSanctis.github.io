Phase-Field Fracture Propagation

A C++ solver for brittle fracture that couples the equilibrium equation with a phase-field damage model on a Material Point Method discretisation.

Type

Personal research project

Period

Spring 2026 — ongoing

Language

C++

Overview

The solver was developed incrementally, from the one-dimensional quasi-static case to the two-dimensional time-dependent one. A spectral decomposition of the elastic energy ensures that only tensile strain drives damage.

The implicit schemes of the reference papers were reformulated into a fully explicit, matrix-free algorithm: an explicit momentum update under a CFL time step, and a damped Jacobi iteration for the elliptic phase-field equation. No global linear solve is needed. A parallel implementation is now in progress.

Results

  • Reproduced the quasi-static single-edge-notched shear test and the dynamic crack branching benchmark from the reference literature.
  • Verified the mechanical solver independently against the analytical shear stiffness, matching it to five significant digits.
