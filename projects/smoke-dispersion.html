Smoke Dispersion from a Vehicle Fire

Simulating the smoke from a bus fire on the Linköping University campus to identify the buildings most at risk.

Institution

Linköping University

Period

Sep — Oct 2026

Compute

NSC Sigma cluster

Overview

Steady RANS simulations (realizable k–ε, 20 million cells) were run on the NSC Sigma HPC cluster through scripted Fluent journals.

Twenty years of hourly wind data were reduced to five representative cases with k-means clustering in MATLAB, and the results were combined into probability-weighted exposure maps for the campus buildings.

Results

  • Compared a reacting combustion model with a passive-scalar model: the simpler model captures the direction of the smoke but not its rise or its chemistry.
  • Co-authored a scientific paper and presented a poster on the work.
